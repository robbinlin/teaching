The Random Component
Identify the response variable and select proper distribution.
In regression, we assume y to be normally distributed.
When y is binary, such as success or failure, we assume a binomial distribution
When y is a count, we assume a Poisson or negative  binomial distribution.

=== Slide 8 ===
The systematic component
The systematic component specifies the explanatory variables.
Typically refers as x’s and appears at ride-hand side of the model equation.
Some x can be based on others in the model. For example, x3=x1*x2, to allow interaction between x1 and x2.
Some x can be the polynomial of the others. For example x4=x1*x1.
X could be both continuous and categorical.
