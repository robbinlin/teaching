=== Slide 35 ===
資料建構與敘述統計
Measure of Centrality
Mean (平均數)
例: 2,3,4,1,5      The mean would be
Median (中位數)
例:1,2,5,8,9       The Median would be 5
例:2,4,5,7,8,9    The Median would be (5+7)/2=6
Mode (眾數)
例:1,2,3,3,9       The mode would be 3
例:1,2,3,3,9,9    The mode would be 3 AND 9
35
[講者備註] 平均數應該是最為常用以衡量中心的統計量，但易受outlier影響
如有資料1,2,3,4,500, the mean would be 102 but the median is 3 
Mode可適用於可類型資料，尤其是nominal
