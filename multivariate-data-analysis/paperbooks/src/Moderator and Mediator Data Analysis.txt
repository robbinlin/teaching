Moderator and Mediator
Moderator: a variable that alters the direction of the relation between a IV and a DV.
Mediator: a variable that explains the relation between a IV and a DV

=== Slide 7 ===
Mediation effect was determined by procedures described by Baron and Kenny (1986). 
Regression 1:if the initial variable significantly predicts the outcome variable. 
Regression 2: if the initial variable significantly predicts the mediator.
Regression 3:both the initial variable and the mediator are entered simultaneously and are used to predict the criterion variable

=== Slide 12 ===
The result indicated that the previously significant relationship between predictor (work hard) and the outcome (tax) becomes non significant (β =-0.05(p=0.15)).
Therefore, there is almost complete mediator effect for Obey in the relationship between work hard and tax.
The Sobel Test indicates that 65.4% of the total effect is mediated by Obey (p<0.001)
